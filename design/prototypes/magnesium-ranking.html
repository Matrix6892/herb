P_unit = price ÷ (servings × magnesium per serving) × 100 mg

Magnesium per serving comes from the label. When the label gives the weight of a compound, we multiply by the share of magnesium in its chemical formula, add the lines exactly and round down once, to the microgram. Commission is not an input of any formula.

Rating adjusted for review count: (n × R + C × m) ÷ (n + C). Balance scores price and rating from 0 to 100 and ranks by the weaker of the two.
