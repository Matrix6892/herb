price per 100 mg = jar price ÷ (servings × magnesium per serving) × 100 mg

Magnesium per serving comes from the label. When the label gives the weight of a compound, we multiply by the share of magnesium in its chemical formula, add the lines exactly and round down once, to the microgram.

Rating is adjusted for the number of reviews: (n × R + C × m) ÷ (n + C). Balance puts each product on a 0 to 100 scale for price per mg (log) and for rating, set by the products compared here, and ranks by the weaker of the two. The score describes this comparison, not the quality of a product.

Commission is not an input of any formula. On the map, the shaded area is the formula of the chosen ranking, drawn: whatever is inside it ranks above the selected product.
